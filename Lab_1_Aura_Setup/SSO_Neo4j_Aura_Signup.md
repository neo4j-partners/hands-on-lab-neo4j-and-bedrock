To access Neo4j Aura Console use incognito mode and do the following steps:

1. Go to https://console-preview.neo4j.io/

3. Click "Continue with organization SSO"
IMAGE: 2_Continue_with_OneBlink_SSO.png


3. Enter org ID from the OneBlink Sign-on Page.
IMAGE:01_One_Blink_Credentials.png

4. Click "Continue with OneBlink SSO"
IMAGE: 3_Continue_with_OneBlink_SSO


5. Enter your login credentials on the Okta page
6. You will be redirected back to the Neo4j Aura Console
